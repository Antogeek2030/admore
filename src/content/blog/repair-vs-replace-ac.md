When a major AC repair is needed, the repair-vs-replace decision depends on age, cost, and long-term efficiency.

## Age of the System

Most central air conditioners last 12–15 years with proper maintenance. Units older than 12 years are often better candidates for replacement, especially if they use older refrigerants.

## Cost of Repair

A common rule of thumb: if the repair cost approaches or exceeds 50% of the price of a new system, replacement usually makes more sense.

## Efficiency and Comfort

Newer systems offer higher SEER ratings, better humidity control, and quieter operation — important advantages in Oklahoma heat and humidity.

## Refrigerant Type

Older R-22 systems are increasingly expensive to service. Transitioning to a modern R-410A or newer refrigerant system can be more economical long-term.
