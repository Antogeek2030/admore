Oklahoma summers put serious demand on air conditioning systems. Preparing your AC in spring can prevent mid-summer breakdowns and lower energy bills.

## Change or Clean Filters

A dirty filter restricts airflow, reduces efficiency, and can cause the evaporator coil to freeze. Check filters monthly during cooling season and replace as needed.

## Clear Outdoor Unit

Keep at least two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser. Remove leaves, grass, and debris so the unit can reject heat properly.

## Schedule a Professional Tune-Up

A licensed technician will check refrigerant levels, clean coils, test electrical components, and verify airflow. Catching small issues early is far cheaper than emergency repairs in July.

## Check Thermostat Settings

Ensure the thermostat is set to cool mode and the temperature is reasonable. Programmable or smart thermostats can reduce runtime when you're away.

## Listen and Look

Unusual noises, weak airflow, or warm air from vents are early warning signs. Address them before peak heat arrives.
