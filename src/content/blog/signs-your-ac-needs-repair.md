Ignoring small AC problems often leads to bigger, more expensive failures. Here are common signs Ardmore homeowners should watch for.

## 1. Warm Air from Vents

If the system runs but air isn't cold, suspect low refrigerant, a failing compressor, or dirty coils.

## 2. Unusual Noises

Grinding, squealing, or banging usually means worn bearings, loose parts, or a struggling motor.

## 3. Frequent Cycling

Short cycling (turning on and off rapidly) can indicate improper sizing, refrigerant issues, or thermostat problems.

## 4. Higher Energy Bills

A sudden spike often means the system is working harder due to restricted airflow or low efficiency.

## 5. Water Leaks or Moisture

Clogged condensate drains or frozen coils can cause water around the indoor unit.

## 6. Weak Airflow

Reduced airflow points to dirty filters, blocked ducts, or blower problems.

## 7. Ice on the Indoor Coil

A frozen evaporator is a clear signal of restricted airflow or low refrigerant — turn the system off and call for service.

If you notice any of these signs, schedule a diagnostic visit. Same-day service is often available in the Ardmore area.
